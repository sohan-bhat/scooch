Everything new since 0.1.0.

Scooch now comes as a desktop app. One window turns Scooch on and off, picks the picture and holds its switches. The first open turns Scooch on. The app brings the `scooch` command along and takes over a copy installed from the command line.

- macOS on Apple silicon and Intel, `Scooch.dmg`
- Windows on x64 or Arm, `Scooch-Setup-x64.exe` or `Scooch-Setup-arm64.exe`
- Linux as a deb, `scooch_amd64.deb` or `scooch_arm64.deb`
- Linux as an rpm, `scooch.x86_64.rpm` or `scooch.aarch64.rpm`
- Linux as an AppImage, `Scooch-x86_64.AppImage` or `Scooch-aarch64.AppImage`

On macOS the app is not notarized, so the first open needs Open Anyway in System Settings under Privacy and Security.

On Windows the setup is not signed, so SmartScreen shows a warning the first time.

On macOS and Windows the picture slides into its new place when an icon moves. The Slide switch or `scooch glide` turns this off.

Clouds gather around your icons on a blue sky, and sand islands on teal or blue water, so the labels sit on calm ground. A picture without a blue sky or blue water gets neither. The Clouds switch or `scooch scenery` turns them off.

On Xfce and on Plasma 5 under X11 the picture moves as soon as you drop a single icon, before the desktop saves its places. The Quick drop switch or `scooch predict` turns this off.

Removing Scooch puts your own wallpaper back and takes the login item and the background process with it. A screen that is unplugged at that moment can keep Scooch's picture when it comes back.

- On macOS move Scooch to the Trash. A move to another folder keeps Scooch on.
- On Windows the uninstaller gives your Path back as it was. Tick its box to delete Scooch's data folder too.
- On Linux remove the deb or the rpm while you are logged in to your desktop, or move the AppImage to the Trash. A login item whose program is gone is removed the next time it runs.

Fixes

- Windows icon boxes are right at 125% display scaling and above, and a focused icon's long label is measured unfolded.
- On macOS Scooch keeps working while the Finder permission window is open and sends Finder nothing until you choose.
- KDE Plasma icons are read on the screen they belong to, Plasma 6 icon sizes are right, and a move is noticed when the mouse button is released.
- Xfce 4.20 icon boxes, Cinnamon 6.8 background lists and GNOME moves are read correctly.
- On Windows, KDE and Xfce the picture follows a moved icon without the quarter second pause.
- On GNOME, Cinnamon and MATE the picture moves as soon as the file manager saves where an icon went.
- On Linux the background process no longer wakes twice a second at rest.
- A damaged entry in Scooch's saved state no longer loses the wallpapers kept for the other screens.

The command alone on macOS and Linux

```sh
curl -LsSf https://sohanbhat.me/scooch/install.sh | sh
```

The command alone on Windows

```powershell
irm https://sohanbhat.me/scooch/install.ps1 | iex
```
