Fixes since 0.1.0.

- Windows icon boxes are right at 125% display scaling and above, and a focused icon's long label is measured unfolded.
- macOS asks for the Finder permission in the background and leaves Finder alone until it is answered.
- KDE Plasma icons are read on the screen they belong to, Plasma 6 icon sizes are right, and a move is noticed when the mouse button is released.
- Xfce 4.20 icon boxes, Cinnamon 6.8 background lists and GNOME moves are read correctly.

What was tested and what was not is in the README.
